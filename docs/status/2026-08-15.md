# Research Status: 2026-08-15

Audience: general research planning
Status: Malaysian long-horizon pediatric HSCT benchmark, not treatment advice

## Question

Does the Malaysian 115-child TDT HSCT cohort close the Southeast Asian
long-term outcome and affordability gaps enough to promote matched-sibling
HSCT or an ATG-containing conditioning regimen beyond benchmark-only status?

## Decision

`promote_malaysia_pediatric_hsct_to_southeast_asian_long_horizon_outcome_and_burden_benchmark_only`

The cohort materially strengthens Southeast Asian long-horizon execution and
observed-burden evidence. It does not justify route or conditioning-regimen
promotion. Matched-sibling HSCT remains
`leading_affordable_curative_route_benchmark_only`.

## What Changed

A reproducible July 1 onward curative-evidence screen found PubMed PMID
`42466977`, which was absent from the repository. PubMed entered the record on
2026-07-17 for a paper published ahead of print on 2026-02-02. The full primary
article is now accessible.

The source adds the repository's first long-horizon pediatric TDT HSCT cohort
from Malaysia: 115 consecutive children, 96 matched-sibling donors, and median
follow-up of 18 years. It also exposes meaningful graft-failure, mortality,
graft-versus-host disease, and veno-occlusive disease burdens.

The source contains material internal reporting conflicts. These prevent its
ATG associations from becoming a causal regimen decision and keep the route at
benchmark-only status.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact - design and denominator:** The retrospective single-center study
  includes 115 consecutive children with TDT who underwent 128 HSCT procedures
  at Universiti Malaya Medical Centre from 1987 through 2023.
- **Fact - donor scope:** Ninety-six children received matched-sibling grafts,
  13 received matched-unrelated umbilical cord blood, and six received
  haploidentical family grafts. Whole-cohort outcomes are not pure
  matched-sibling rates.
- **Fact - disease scope:** The cohort included 97 children with homozygous
  beta-thalassemia, 16 with hemoglobin E-beta-thalassemia, and two with
  hemoglobin H-Constant Spring.
- **Fact - long-horizon outcome:** At median follow-up of 18 years, range 1 to
  36, the full cohort's reported 15-year overall survival was 83.5% and
  thalassemia-free survival was 71.3%. Table 2 reports matched-sibling estimates
  of 86.5% and 72.9%, without a calendar-era split or number-at-risk table.
- **Fact - mortality and graft failure:** The paper reports 18
  transplant-related deaths, 14 within 100 days. It reports 30 graft failures,
  including 17 primary and 13 secondary failures.
- **Fact - toxicity:** Grade II to IV acute graft-versus-host disease occurred
  in 30 children, grade III to IV disease in 10, and chronic
  graft-versus-host disease in 10. Veno-occlusive disease occurred in 32
  children, including seven severe events.
- **Fact - conditioning:** Every participant received myeloablative
  conditioning. Table 1 lists 62 busulfan-cyclophosphamide recipients without
  ATG, 32 with ATG, and 21 people in other ATG-containing regimens. No
  conditioning pharmacokinetic monitoring was available. ATG use was based
  largely on physician preference.

## Contradictions And Limits

- The graft-failure section says 30 of 155 patients experienced graft failure,
  while the study cohort is 115 and 30 corresponds to the reported 26.1% only
  against 115. Treat `155` as an unresolved source error.
- The abstract says that 32 of 62 busulfan-cyclophosphamide recipients had ATG,
  while Table 1 lists 62 busulfan-cyclophosphamide recipients and a separate
  32-person busulfan-cyclophosphamide-ATG group.
- The graft-failure prose prints the upper confidence limit for the no-ATG
  estimate as `0.506`. Table 3 and the abstract report `40.506` and `40.51`.
- The acute graft-versus-host disease prose combines the marrow-source
  matched-sibling estimate, RR 2.001 with `p=0.211`, with the
  donor-relationship matched-sibling result, RR 0.112 with `p=0.043`.
- Table 1 labels the chelation-therapy section `n=63`, while its three rows total
  115 and use full-cohort percentages. The following chelation-history rows do
  total 63. The first label is therefore unresolved.
- The 36-year enrollment span mixes HLA-typing methods, donor sources,
  conditioning regimens, supportive care, risk groups, and calendar eras.
- The paper reports no regimen-aligned infection denominator, current-era
  matched-sibling outcome, complete late endocrine, fertility, malignancy, or
  organ outcome, local HSCT cost, payer payment, or center-capacity denominator.

## Cure-Program Read

- **Mechanism and scope:** Myeloablative conditioning followed by donor HSPC
  infusion replaces ineffective recipient erythropoiesis. The observed scope
  is a mixed-donor, mixed-regimen pediatric TDT cohort, not a current Indonesian
  program or pure matched-sibling route.
- **Evidence tier:** Direct human outcome evidence from a retrospective,
  single-center, nonrandomized clinical cohort spanning 36 years.
- **Hypothesis:** A current, standardized matched-sibling marrow program in
  Southeast Asia with regimen-specific pharmacokinetic monitoring can improve
  graft failure and early mortality relative to this mixed-era benchmark while
  retaining a delivered-cost advantage over individualized autologous
  gene-cell therapy.
- **Interpretation:** Promote the source only to a Southeast Asian long-horizon
  outcome and observed-burden benchmark. Do not promote ATG causal benefit, a
  current matched-sibling outcome rate, safety advantage, affordability,
  Indonesia access, or individual suitability.
- **Open question:** In a consecutive current-era matched-sibling subgroup,
  what are the number-at-risk, regimen, pharmacokinetic, graft-failure,
  mortality, infection, graft-versus-host disease, endocrine, fertility,
  malignancy, organ, quality-of-life, and itemized-cost outcomes?
- **Confidence:** High that this is a material Southeast Asian execution and
  long-horizon outcome source. Moderate for the full-cohort outcome and burden
  estimates. Low for causal ATG benefit, current matched-sibling transfer,
  affordability, Indonesia access, or payer conclusions.
- **Affordability and access:** The article cites external Malaysian standard-
  care cost literature but reports no HSCT delivery cost or aligned gene-cell
  comparator. It does not establish Indonesia authorization, routine access,
  capacity, payment, or suitability.

## Falsification Criterion

Remove the benchmark if a publisher or author correction materially changes
the 115-child denominator or the reported 15-year outcomes. Keep the ATG
association blocked unless corrected, era-adjusted and regimen-aligned analysis
reproduces lower graft failure and acute graft-versus-host disease without an
unacceptable infection, mortality, or cost penalty. Remove the leading
affordable-route label if a current matched-sibling cohort fails the existing
durability, severe-toxicity, or lifetime delivered-cost thresholds.

## Next Decisive Action

Obtain author or publisher correction for the 155 denominator, the ATG group
denominator, the graft-failure confidence interval, the chelation label, and
the acute graft-versus-host disease regression description. Then request
current-era matched-sibling number-at-risk, regimen, pharmacokinetic,
graft-failure, mortality, late-safety, and itemized-cost fields. Compare those
fields with the 176-child LMIC cohort and the two-case Indonesia execution
benchmark. No patient action, referral, or sample routing follows.
