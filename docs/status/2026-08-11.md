# Research Status: 2026-08-11

Audience: general research planning
Status: long-term clinical durability benchmark, not treatment advice

## Question

Does the 63-participant long-term betibeglogene autotemcel study close the
durability, safety, and affordability gaps enough to promote beti-cel from a
clinical benchmark to an affordable curative route for transfusion-dependent
beta-thalassemia?

## Decision

`promote_beti_cel_to_durable_long_term_transfusion_independence_benchmark_only`

The study materially strengthens the durability evidence for lentiviral
beta-globin gene addition. It does not establish an affordable route. Full
busulfan myeloablation, apheresis, individualized manufacturing, early serious
toxicity, fertility risk, long-term malignancy surveillance, absent delivered
cost, and unresolved Indonesia access keep route-level promotion blocked.
Matched-sibling HSCT remains
`leading_affordable_curative_route_benchmark_only`.

## What Changed

The repository had captured PMID `41525466` in an April PubMed snapshot but had
not integrated its long-term outcomes into the canonical curative radar or
promotion decision. The peer-reviewed Blood paper pools 63 treated participants
from four nonrandomized parent studies who entered long-term follow-up. It adds
a median 5.9 years of follow-up, with 51 participants followed for at least five
years and two followed for ten years.

This is decision-changing for evidence tier, not affordability. Beti-cel now
provides the program's clearest long-term autologous gene-addition durability
benchmark. It still does not compete with matched-sibling HSCT on documented
delivered cost, local capacity, or Indonesia access.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact - design and denominator:** The peer-reviewed primary paper, DOI
  `10.1182/blood.2025029196` and PMID `41525466`, reports 63 people with TDT who
  received beti-cel in phase 1/2 studies, `n = 22`, or phase 3 studies,
  `n = 41`, and entered prospective long-term follow-up. Median age was 17
  years, range 4 to 35. Median follow-up was 5.9 years, range 2.9 to 10.1.
- **Fact - transfusion independence:** Fifteen of 22 phase 1/2 participants,
  68.2%, and 37 of 41 phase 3 participants, 90.2%, achieved transfusion
  independence and sustained it through their last follow-up. The weighted
  average hemoglobin during transfusion independence was 10.2 g/dL in phase
  1/2 and 11.2 g/dL in phase 3.
- **Fact - biological and iron endpoints:** The paper reports stable peripheral
  blood vector copy number and beta-A-T87Q hemoglobin from month six through
  last follow-up. Thirty-eight of the 52 participants who achieved transfusion
  independence, 73%, had stopped iron chelation at last follow-up without an
  increase in liver iron concentration. Reported markers of ineffective
  erythropoiesis and health-related quality of life also improved.
- **Fact - observed long-term safety:** No malignancy, insertional oncogenesis,
  or vector-derived replication-competent lentivirus was reported at the
  February 2024 cutoff. No beti-cel-related serious adverse event was reported
  more than two years after infusion.
- **Fact - regulatory process and early safety:** The current FDA product page
  still lists Zynteglo for adults and children with beta-thalassemia who require
  regular red-cell transfusions. Its package insert requires mobilization,
  apheresis, backup-cell collection, individualized manufacture and testing,
  full busulfan myeloablation, and at least 15 years of annual malignancy
  monitoring. In the separate 41-person FDA safety cohort with median 27.2
  months of follow-up, serious adverse reactions occurred in 37%. Serious
  hepatic veno-occlusive disease occurred in three participants. The label
  states that its adverse-event tables include events associated with busulfan
  conditioning, so those events cannot be assigned to beti-cel alone.
- **Fact - current registry state:** `NCT02633943`, study identifier `LTF-303`,
  is active but not recruiting, lists 66 actual enrollees, estimates completion
  in 2035, and has no posted results. The 66-person registry enrollment is not
  the paper's treated or outcome denominator and must not be substituted for
  the 63 participants analyzed at the paper cutoff.
- **Fact - Indonesia public-record screen:** On 2026-08-11, exact `ZYNTEGLO`
  and `betibeglogene` product-name and ingredient searches returned zero public
  BPOM medicine records. This is a bounded public-database result, not proof of
  no authorization, special access, hospital procurement, availability, or
  patient eligibility.

## Contradictions And Limits

- The phrase "up to ten years" applies to two of 63 participants. Fifty-one
  participants had at least five years of follow-up. It must not be presented
  as ten-year evidence for the entire cohort.
- The phase 3 process produced higher transduction efficiency, drug-product
  vector copy number, beta-A-T87Q hemoglobin, and transfusion-independence rates
  than the earlier phase 1/2 process. The pooled program therefore contains a
  manufacturing-version difference, not one uniform product process across all
  63 participants.
- The study is a pooled follow-up of nonrandomized, open-label, single-dose
  parent trials without a concurrent HSCT, exa-cel, or standard-care comparator.
  It establishes within-cohort durability, not comparative superiority.
- No observed malignancy at median 5.9 years narrows the current safety signal
  but does not clear insertional-oncogenesis risk. The FDA label retains a
  potential-risk warning and long-term surveillance requirement, while
  `LTF-303` remains incomplete and has no posted registry results.
- The paper was funded by Genetix Biotherapeutics, formerly bluebird bio. The
  sponsor contributed to study design, conceptualization, data interpretation,
  and medical writing. This does not invalidate the findings, but it limits
  independence and strengthens the need for complete long-term denominators.

## Cure-Program Read

- **Hypothesis:** Durable autologous beta-globin gene addition could become a
  realistic affordable route only if the phase 3-like manufacturing process can
  be transferred with stable efficacy, low failed-batch and repeat-collection
  rates, safer or less resource-intensive conditioning, and a total delivered
  cost and capacity advantage over exa-cel while approaching the
  matched-sibling HSCT benchmark.
- **Interpretation:** Promote beti-cel's evidence tier to a durable long-term
  transfusion-independence benchmark. Do not promote it as affordable, locally
  accessible, safer than HSCT or exa-cel, or suitable for any individual.
- **Open question:** What are the denominator-aligned manufacturing-failure,
  repeat-collection, bridge-care, admission, complication, fertility,
  surveillance, and payer costs for a phase 3-like beti-cel route in an Asian
  delivery setting?
- **Confidence:** High that beti-cel can produce durable transfusion independence
  in many participants represented by these selected trials. Moderate for the
  absence of a major late vector safety signal at the current cutoff. Low for
  comparative safety, transferability, affordability, capacity, and Indonesia
  access.
- **Affordability and access:** The FDA label describes a target collection of
  at least 12 million CD34-positive cells per kilogram, possible repeat
  collection and manufacture, backup-cell storage, full busulfan conditioning,
  hospital care, and approximately 70 to 90 days for manufacture and testing.
  The long-term paper reports no itemized delivered cost, failed-batch
  denominator, Indonesia authorization, qualified-center capacity, technology
  transfer, or payer coverage.

## Falsification Criterion

Deprioritize beti-cel as an affordable-route candidate if a denominator-aligned
implementation study using the phase 3-like process fails to preserve durable
transfusion independence, reveals unacceptable clonal or malignant risk,
cannot reduce conditioning and manufacturing burden, or cannot show a credible
total-cost and capacity advantage over exa-cel while approaching the
matched-sibling HSCT benchmark. Any confirmed insertional oncogenesis,
vector-derived replication-competent lentivirus, material loss of
transfusion independence, or nonrecoverable manufacturing-failure pattern must
trigger immediate re-ranking.

## Next Decisive Action

A qualified gene-therapy manufacturing and transplant owner should provide one
denominator-aligned implementation dataset for a phase 3-like beti-cel process:
attempted collections, repeat collections, released and failed lots, collection
to release time, bridge transfusions, busulfan exposure, admission days,
intensive-care and VOD events, fertility preservation, readmissions, long-term
surveillance, and itemized payer-relevant cost. Compare the same activity and
cost fields with exa-cel and matched-sibling HSCT. Keep the route at benchmark
only if those denominators cannot be obtained. No patient action, referral, or
sample routing is authorized.
