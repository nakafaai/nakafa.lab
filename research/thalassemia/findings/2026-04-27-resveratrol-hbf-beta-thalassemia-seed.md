# Finding: Resveratrol HbF And Iron-Overload Evidence

Date checked: 2026-08-01
Evidence label: human NTDT comparator and preclinical HbF seed, not treatment
advice

## Question And Decision

**Question:** Does the 2026 randomized Thailand ferritin result, read with the
earlier human resveratrol trial, justify keeping purified resveratrol in the
first affordable-cure HbF quote panel?

**Decision:**
`deprioritize_resveratrol_from_first_affordable_cure_hbf_panel_to_ntdt_iron_overload_comparator_only`.

The new result is decision-relevant, but it reports a six-month serum-ferritin
signal in 10 resveratrol recipients, not HbF, hemoglobin, transfusion burden,
organ iron, or transfusion independence. The earlier randomized NTDT study used
resveratrol with piperine and did not establish a decision-useful HbF or
transfusion effect. Do not spend first-quote capacity on purified resveratrol.

**Confidence:** High that current human evidence does not support active
affordable-cure or HbF-panel priority. Low to moderate that the new ferritin
signal represents lower tissue iron because the conference abstract is small,
serum ferritin is an indirect marker, and registered iron biomarkers and organ
iron were not reported.

## Direct Evidence

| Source | Fact | Decision relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PMID `22378234`, 2012 | Patient-derived erythroid precursor Table II reports seven beta-thalassemia participants tested with 40 micromolar resveratrol. Baseline HbF ranged from 1.97% to 21.15% and post-exposure HbF from 5.85% to 39.27%. | Direct cell signal, but not a controlled clinical outcome. The paper also uses conflicting 20, 40, and 100 micromolar descriptions across the text, table, and figure caption. |
| PMID `23975182`, 2014 | Five micromolar resveratrol accelerated maturation and activated FoxO3 in human CD34-positive erythroid cultures, including 20 cultures from 10 people with beta-thalassemia intermedia. Six-month mouse treatment improved anemia-related phenotypes in `Hbbth3/+` mice. | Supports an erythroid mechanism only. It does not establish human HbF benefit, transfusion independence, safety, or durability. |
| PMID `29926158`, 2018 | A double-blind single-center trial randomized 54 adults with NTDT to hydroxyurea, resveratrol plus piperine, or their combination for six months. The abstract reports no significant response-rate difference. | Human comparator evidence exists, so the former no-trial statement was incomplete. The formulation does not isolate purified resveratrol. |
| Cochrane review, PMID `36637054`, 2023 | For hydroxyurea versus resveratrol, hemoglobin mean difference was -0.30 g/dL, 95% CI -1.14 to 0.54, in 34 participants, with low-certainty evidence. Three participants required a transfusion, but frequency and interval were not reported. HbF was not reported for this comparison. | The confidence interval includes benefit and harm, and the trial does not supply the HbF or transfusion-burden evidence needed for cure-program promotion. |
| EHA-3064, 2026 | A randomized, double-blind trial enrolled 30 adults with NTDT on stable iron chelation: resveratrol `n=10`, omeprazole `n=11`, placebo `n=9`. Median six-month ferritin change was -116 ng/mL with resveratrol, -8 with omeprazole, and +8 with placebo. Resveratrol differed from omeprazole (`P=0.016`) and placebo (`P=0.012`). The resveratrol group reported three adverse events. | This is a small conference-abstract iron signal. It does not report HbF, hemoglobin, transfusion burden, liver iron concentration, labile plasma iron, non-transferrin-bound iron, chelation changes, durability, or cost. |
| ISRCTN `73258526` | The prospectively registered protocol planned 60 participants and secondary measures including labile plasma iron, non-transferrin-bound iron, erythroferrone, hepcidin, and association with HbF. The registry remained recruiting or ongoing when checked. | The 30-person conference report and 60-person registry target are unresolved. The unreported prespecified outcomes are the next evidence trigger. |

## Contradictions And Boundaries

- **Cohort mismatch:** The registry target is 60, while the conference abstract
  reports 30 enrolled participants. The registry does not explain whether the
  abstract is interim, early-stopped, or a complete cohort.
- **Endpoint mismatch:** The registered study includes iron biomarkers and an
  HbF association, but the conference abstract reports ferritin and adverse
  events only.
- **Material mismatch:** The 2018 human trial tested resveratrol with piperine.
  It cannot validate a purified-resveratrol quote item.
- **Concentration mismatch:** The 2012 primary paper describes 20, 40, and 100
  micromolar exposures in different patient-cell passages. A single clinical or
  assay concentration cannot be reconstructed reliably from that report.
- **Population boundary:** The randomized human studies concern NTDT, and the
  2026 abstract reports that only half the cohort had beta-thalassemia. These
  data do not establish an effect in TDT.
- **Safety boundary:** Six months and 10 resveratrol recipients cannot establish
  uncommon toxicity or long-term safety.

## Cure-Program Implications

- **Fact:** Resveratrol has patient-cell and randomized human thalassemia
  evidence, but no controlled evidence here establishes durable transfusion
  independence.
- **Hypothesis:** The 2026 ferritin signal reflects an iron-axis adjunct effect
  distinct from HbF induction. It would become more credible only if liver iron
  or the registered labile-iron biomarkers improve with stable chelation and no
  worsening of hemoglobin or transfusion burden.
- **Interpretation:** Retain resveratrol only as an NTDT iron-overload comparator
  while the complete registered outcomes remain unavailable. Remove it from the
  current first affordable-cure HbF quote.
- **Open question:** Does the complete randomized result reconcile the cohort
  size and show organ-iron or labile-iron improvement without anemia,
  transfusion, or safety tradeoffs?
- **Affordability and access:** The conference and registry sources report no
  itemized product, chelation, monitoring, adherence, adverse-event, or total
  delivered cost. An orally administered research intervention is not proof of
  affordability, availability, or an Indonesia access path.
- **Falsification criterion:** Reconsider HbF-panel priority only if a complete
  controlled report shows a reproducible HbF-protein or F-cell effect plus
  hemoglobin or transfusion benefit, acceptable safety, durability, and
  delivered cost. If the complete iron study does not reproduce ferritin with
  liver-iron or labile-iron improvement, deprioritize the iron-comparator lane
  too.
- **Next decisive action:** Monitor the EHA report and ISRCTN record for a full
  result that reconciles enrollment and reports HbF, hemoglobin, transfusion
  outcomes, liver iron or labile-iron biomarkers, chelation stability, complete
  adverse events, follow-up, and itemized delivered cost. Do not add a new
  resveratrol lab quote before that result.
