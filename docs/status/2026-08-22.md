# Research Status: 2026-08-22

Audience: general research planning
Status: Astatine-211 conditioning held at a nonhuman-primate benchmark after Indonesia production screening

## Question

Does current official radionuclide-production evidence establish an Indonesian
Astatine-211 route strongly enough to treat anti-CD45 radioimmunotherapy as a
realistically affordable TDT conditioning strategy?

## Decision

`hold_astatine_211_cd45_rit_at_nonhuman_primate_targeted_conditioning_benchmark_only_after_indonesia_production_screen`

The large-animal conditioning benchmark remains scientifically relevant, but
the affordable-route hypothesis is not supported by a public Indonesian
production path. Matched-sibling HSCT remains
`leading_affordable_curative_route_benchmark_only` for its narrow donor and risk
scope.

## What Changed

The August 21 review established nonhuman-primate engraftment feasibility but
left isotope access unresolved. A targeted official-source screen now makes
that gap concrete. A 2025 BRIN infrastructure review states that Astatine-211
requires an alpha beam and that the three Indonesian hospital cyclotrons
operating through the end of 2024 produced only fluorine-18. The current IAEA
directory lists four Indonesian machines with proton energies from 9.6 to 18
MeV and blank alpha-energy and alpha-current fields. Recent BAPETEN verification
records show additional cyclotron development, but the named production scope
remains fluorine-18 FDG rather than Astatine-211. A second official IAEA
production report resolves the capability criterion more precisely and exposes
a threshold conflict that the first screen did not record.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact - production requirement:** The 2025 BRIN review identifies
  Astatine-211 as an alpha-particle production case with a reported threshold of
  19.36 MeV. IAEA Radioisotopes and Radiopharmaceuticals Reports No. 4 instead
  gives a 20.718 MeV reaction threshold and a 21.0 to 28.6 MeV practical window
  for the bismuth-209(alpha,2n)Astatine-211 route. It limits alpha energy to
  about 28 MeV to avoid the radiotoxic polonium-210 impurity pathway associated
  with astatine-210 co-production. The same report gives a 7.214-hour half-life,
  which makes production and time-to-use logistics part of the route rather
  than a separable procurement detail.
- **Fact - Indonesian operating baseline:** BRIN reports that, through the end
  of 2024, the three hospital cyclotrons serving Indonesian nuclear medicine
  were at Dharmais Cancer Hospital, MRCCC Siloam Hospital, and Gading Pluit
  Hospital. It states that all three produced only fluorine-18. The same review
  describes 13, 16.5, 24, and 30 MeV development projects without identifying
  an Astatine-211 production line, alpha-beam capability, or released product.
- **Fact - current IAEA public directory:** The live IAEA country filter
  returned four Indonesian entries. Their listed proton energies are 11, 18,
  18, and 9.6 MeV. All four alpha-energy and alpha-current fields are blank.
  Three records were last modified in 2018 or 2019 and one in 2023.
- **Fact - newer regulator evidence:** BAPETEN's April 2026 verification at PT
  Kardia Farma Solusindo names an IBA Cyclone KIUBE 300 and fluorine-18 FDG
  production. Separate 2025 verifications at RSUD Abdul Wahab Sjahranie and PT
  Global Onkolab Farma also name cyclotrons and fluorine-18 production. These
  are verification records, not public Astatine-211 license decisions or
  evidence of released Astatine-211 batches.
- **Fact - clinical boundary:** An exact ClinicalTrials.gov search combining
  thalassemia aliases with Astatine-211 and BC8 aliases returned zero studies on
  2026-08-22. `NCT04083183` remains a mixed nonmalignant-disease allogeneic
  transplant comparator, not a TDT or Indonesian production result.

## Contradictions And Limits

- BRIN reports a 19.36 MeV threshold, while the IAEA production report gives a
  20.718 MeV reaction threshold and a 21.0 to 28.6 MeV practical window. The
  source values should not be merged. The facility gate now uses the IAEA
  practical window and impurity-control ceiling rather than treating either
  threshold as sufficient operating capability.
- The IAEA directory is explicitly under revision and its Indonesian records
  are not current enough to prove that no newer or unlisted machine has alpha
  capability. Blank alpha fields are bounded public-directory evidence, not
  proof of physical absence.
- BRIN's infrastructure inventory is current only through the end of 2024.
  BAPETEN records confirm subsequent facility development, but the reviewed
  pages name fluorine-18 scope and do not expose a complete national license
  inventory or every technical beam specification.
- A BAPETEN verification visit does not by itself establish that an operating
  license was issued, that routine production followed, or that a facility can
  add another radionuclide without further technical, quality, and regulatory
  work.
- General Indonesian rules and release limits that mention Astatine-211 do not
  establish a licensed producer, product, batch, antibody-radiolabeling route,
  clinical supply, payer path, or affordability.

## Cure-Program Read

- **Mechanism and scope:** Astatine-211-labeled anti-CD45 is intended to open
  marrow space for transplanted HSPCs. The new evidence concerns isotope
  production infrastructure, not any TDT genotype, phenotype, efficacy, or
  patient population.
- **Evidence tier:** Official national research review, live IAEA infrastructure
  directory, official Indonesian regulator verification records, and official
  production guidance. This is infrastructure and access evidence, not a
  treatment-outcome study.
- **Hypothesis:** A local cyclotron qualified for the 21.0 to 28.6 MeV practical
  alpha window, bismuth-target recovery line, antibody radiolabeling process,
  impurity control, and same-day release workflow could make the isotope
  component reproducible enough to compare with busulfan conditioning.
- **Interpretation:** Hold Astatine-211 anti-CD45 at the nonhuman-primate
  conditioning benchmark. The public Indonesian evidence does not support an
  affordable or accessible production route.
- **Open question:** Is there a named Indonesian facility with a verified 21.0
  to 28.6 MeV alpha operating window and current, bismuth-target handling,
  Astatine-211 recovery and radiolabeling, sterile release testing,
  astatine-210 and polonium-210 impurity control, batch yield, and a
  regulator-accepted production scope?
- **Safety risk:** The marrow-niche, organ, fertility, clonal, and malignancy
  risks from the August 21 review remain unresolved. Production adds radiation
  protection, impurity, release, waste, and time-to-use failure risks.
- **Affordability and access:** No source provides an Indonesian Astatine-211
  batch, yield, failed-batch denominator, transport time, radiolabeled-antibody
  release result, delivered cost, payer route, or comparison with busulfan.
  Current positive evidence is fluorine-18 infrastructure, not a transferable
  Astatine-211 manufacturing result.
- **Confidence:** High that the reviewed public Indonesian sources do not
  establish an Astatine-211 production route. Moderate that the named existing
  and newer facilities are oriented to proton-produced fluorine-18. Low for the
  stronger claim that no unlisted, upgraded, or planned alpha-capable facility
  exists.

## Falsification Criterion

End the Indonesian affordable-route hypothesis if no named facility can verify
the required 21.0 to 28.6 MeV alpha operating window, bismuth-target and
recovery process, antibody radiolabeling, sterile and radionuclidic release,
astatine-210 and polonium-210 impurity control, reproducible batch yield, and
same-day logistics, or if activity-based delivered cost is not lower than the
conditioning cost and infrastructure burden it is intended to replace.

## Next Decisive Action

The radiopharmacy and health-economics owners should obtain one public-safe
facility capability record with alpha-particle type, the qualified 21.0 to 28.6
MeV operating window and current, target and recovery system, Astatine-211 and
antibody-labeling permissions, astatine-210 and polonium-210 impurity controls,
quality-control and release workflow, successful and failed batch denominators,
hours from bombardment to infusion, waste handling, and activity-based cost.
Only then should the route enter a contemporaneous busulfan cost and safety
comparison. No patient action, treatment recommendation, referral, purchase,
or sample-routing instruction follows.
