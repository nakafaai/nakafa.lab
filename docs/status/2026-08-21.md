# Research Status: 2026-08-21

Audience: general research planning
Status: Astatine-211 anti-CD45 conditioning promoted to a nonhuman-primate benchmark only

## Question

Does the peer-reviewed Astatine-211 anti-CD45 radioimmunotherapy study close
the conditioning toxicity and engraftment gap enough to promote a TDT
affordable-curative route?

## Decision

`promote_astatine_211_cd45_rit_to_nonhuman_primate_targeted_conditioning_benchmark_only`

The route is promoted from an untracked concept to a large-animal targeted
conditioning benchmark. It is not promoted as a TDT cure, a clinically safer
busulfan replacement, or an affordable or accessible route. Matched-sibling
HSCT remains `leading_affordable_curative_route_benchmark_only` for its narrow
donor and risk scope.

## What Changed

A peer-reviewed four-animal rhesus-macaque study now reports durable engraftment
of multiplex-edited autologous HSPCs after Astatine-211-labeled humanized
anti-CD45 conditioning. A separate peer-reviewed mouse study shows that the
same target class can substantially deplete and remodel the marrow endothelial
niche. Reading the studies together resolves an apparent safety contradiction:
durable blood-cell engraftment and an absence of obvious organ toxicity in a
small macaque cohort do not establish marrow-microenvironment safety.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact - mechanism and population:** PMID `42224345` tested
  Astatine-211-labeled humanized anti-CD45 antibody `HuBC8-B10` in four healthy
  rhesus macaques. Two received 300 microcuries per kilogram and two received
  400 microcuries per kilogram. Mobilized autologous CD34-positive HSPCs were
  edited ex vivo at the HBG promoters to reactivate HbF and at CD33, then
  infused 72 hours after radioimmunotherapy. This was a conditioning model, not
  a TDT genotype or disease-phenotype study.
- **Fact - endpoint:** The investigators report dose-dependent engraftment,
  stable multilineage contribution for more than 18 months, and up to 70%
  combined monoallelic and biallelic editing in blood by single-cell analysis.
  Neutrophil and platelet recovery was compared with historical total-body
  irradiation or busulfan controls, not a contemporaneous randomized busulfan
  group.
- **Fact - observed burden:** All four macaques had weight loss and poor
  appetite, with weight recovery within 50 days. Liver tests were reported as
  unaffected. Nonhematopoietic histology at 12 months was reported for one
  animal at the higher dose. The authors state that four animals and the
  available follow-up cannot fully define long-term safety.
- **Fact - marrow-niche safety:** PMID `41671449` compared 20-microcurie
  anti-CD45 radioimmunotherapy, isotype radioimmunotherapy, and 500-centigray
  total-body irradiation in adult immunocompetent mice. Main hematopoietic
  analyses used 7 to 9 mice per condition and time point through day 28. The
  anti-CD45 group had delayed marrow recovery, more than tenfold fewer marrow
  cells at day 7, nearly tenfold fewer long-term HSC-enriched cells at day 14,
  reduced colony output, and persistent endothelial-niche remodeling.
- **Fact - clinical boundary:** `NCT04083183` is a recruiting phase 1/2 study
  in nonmalignant diseases, but it combines Astatine-211 anti-CD45 with
  fludarabine, cyclophosphamide, antithymocyte globulin, total-body irradiation,
  and allogeneic HCT. It has no posted results and cannot isolate an
  antibody-only or autologous effect. An exact ClinicalTrials.gov search found
  no thalassemia record naming astatine, `211At`, or `BC8-B10`.

## Contradiction And Limits

- The macaque paper's statement that no significant treatment-related toxicity
  occurred is an investigator interpretation from four healthy animals. The
  reported weight and appetite effects, one-animal 12-month tissue assessment,
  historical controls, and limited follow-up do not establish comparative or
  complete safety.
- The mouse niche study does not directly refute the macaque engraftment
  result. Myeloablation is the intended mechanism, and the studies differ in
  species, dose, comparator, follow-up, and endpoint. It does show that blood
  recovery and routine organ monitoring can miss endothelial and stromal
  injury relevant to long-term marrow function.
- The macaque HBG edit was a monitoring model in healthy animals. It does not
  establish correction of ineffective erythropoiesis, alpha and beta globin
  chain balance, hemolysis, iron burden, or transfusion independence in any TDT
  genotype or phenotype.
- The paper used a 29-MeV alpha-beam cyclotron, a bismuth target, wet-chemistry
  isotope isolation, radiolabeling, mobilization, apheresis, ex vivo editing,
  and autologous infusion. Removing busulfan from this animal protocol does not
  remove specialized manufacturing or care infrastructure.
- The authors disclose consulting, ownership, research-support, and clinical
  trial-support relationships. Four authors filed a patent application related
  to radioimmunotherapy with humanized BC8. The result is investigator-led and
  not an independent replication.

## Cure-Program Read

- **Mechanism and scope:** A short-range alpha emitter attached to anti-CD45 is
  intended to deplete CD45-positive hematopoietic cells and open marrow space
  for edited autologous HSPCs. The current evidence scope is healthy rhesus
  macaques plus mechanistic mouse safety, not any TDT genotype or phenotype.
- **Evidence tier:** Peer-reviewed large-animal preclinical evidence with four
  treated animals, two doses, historical controls, and more than 18 months of
  engraftment follow-up, supported by peer-reviewed short-term mouse
  marrow-niche evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** Targeted alpha radioimmunotherapy could preserve durable
  edited-HSPC engraftment while reducing the off-target tissue injury caused by
  conventional genotoxic conditioning.
- **Interpretation:** Promote the route to a nonhuman-primate targeted
  conditioning benchmark only. The current studies support biological
  feasibility and a safety question worth testing, not clinical superiority,
  TDT efficacy, cure, affordability, access, or individual suitability.
- **Open question:** At equivalent durable engraftment, does Astatine-211
  anti-CD45 improve marrow endothelial, stromal, organ, fertility, endocrine,
  clonal, and malignancy safety relative to busulfan in a contemporaneous
  controlled study?
- **Safety risk:** Marrow-niche injury, radiation exposure, delayed or
  incomplete hematopoietic recovery, organ and fertility effects, clonal
  selection, and secondary malignancy remain unresolved. The clinical registry
  records do not supply a TDT result.
- **Affordability and access:** The route may avoid conventional busulfan, but
  it adds isotope production, radiochemistry, dosimetry, radiation-medicine
  controls, and the existing autologous collection and editing workflow. No
  delivered cost, payer path, production-yield denominator, Indonesia capacity,
  or access result is reported.
- **Confidence:** High that the route achieved durable edited-cell engraftment
  in the reported four-animal model. Moderate that marrow-niche injury is a
  route-relevant hazard requiring direct comparison. Low for TDT efficacy,
  comparative clinical safety, long-term risk, affordability, and access.

## Falsification Criterion

Deprioritize Astatine-211 anti-CD45 as a busulfan-alternative benchmark if a
contemporaneous study using the same edited autologous HSPC product fails to
match durable multilineage engraftment or fails to improve a prespecified
marrow-niche and nonhematopoietic safety composite relative to busulfan.
Further deprioritize the route if longer follow-up identifies persistent niche
failure, organ or fertility injury, clonal dominance, malignancy, or an isotope
production and delivery burden that prevents a credible lower-cost path.

## Next Decisive Action

The translational conditioning evidence owner, together with radiopharmacy and
HSPC-manufacturing owners, should design a contemporaneous controlled
nonhuman-primate comparison of Astatine-211 anti-CD45 and busulfan using the same
HBG-edited autologous HSPC product and cell dose. Prespecify durable multilineage
engraftment, marrow endothelial and stromal recovery, blood-count and
transfusion burden, organ, fertility and endocrine injury, dosimetry, clonal
composition, malignancy surveillance, batch failure, and activity-based
delivered cost. No patient action, treatment recommendation, referral, or
sample-routing instruction follows.
