# Daily Research Journal: 2026-07-25

Audience: founder / researcher
Status: HGI-001 now has source-linked early clinical evidence but remains held

## Question And Decision

**Question:** Does the source-linked five-person HGI-001 paper justify promoting
lentiviral gene addition as an affordable Asian cure route?

**Decision:**
`hold_hgi001_at_early_clinical_benchmark_only_pending_larger_cohort_safety_durability_and_cost`.

The evidence tier rises from an unlinked registry narrative to a
peer-reviewed five-child pilot. The small conflicted evidence base, busulfan
route, incomplete long-term safety, and absent delivered cost keep the gene-cell
route ranking unchanged.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact and source link:** PMID `41693207` reports hemobeglogene autotemcel, or
  hemo-cel. Hemogen identifies hemo-cel as HGI-001. BGI's publication page links
  the paper to `NCT04592458`, which ClinicalTrials.gov lists as an alias of
  `NCT05745532`.
- **Fact and population:** The single-center, single-arm pilot treated five
  children with transfusion-dependent beta-thalassemia, including three with
  beta-zero/beta-zero genotypes.
- **Fact and endpoints:** The abstract reports median follow-up of 34.7 months.
  All five remained transfusion independent for more than 26 months, with a
  median of 34.3 months. Median neutrophil and platelet engraftment occurred on
  days 18 and 13. HbA-T87Q and vector copy number were sustained.
- **Fact and safety observation:** The abstract reports no unexpected adverse
  events, clonal dominance, or replication-competent lentivirus during the
  reported follow-up.
- **Evidence tier:** Peer-reviewed early clinical outcome evidence, not a
  randomized, comparative, independent, phase 2, affordability, or access
  result.

## Contradiction Resolved And Conflicts Preserved

- **Resolved provenance gap:** The five-person clinical signal is source-linked.
  The two-person preprint is an earlier data cut and must not be counted as two
  additional participants.
- **Unresolved phase and denominator conflict:** The paper calls the study phase
  1/2 and reports five treated participants. The aliased ClinicalTrials.gov
  record labels it early phase 1, estimates ten participants, remains
  recruiting, and has no posted results. Planned, treated, and outcome-evaluable
  denominators remain separate.
- **Unresolved duration conflict:** The new phase 2 narrative says four of five
  participants remained transfusion independent for more than two years. The
  paper abstract says all five did so for more than 26 months. Different cutoffs
  may explain the mismatch, but the reviewed sources do not resolve it.
- **Safety wording correction:** The phase 2 narrative says no severe adverse
  events. The paper abstract says no unexpected adverse events, and BGI's
  publication summary says serious effects were mainly associated with
  busulfan conditioning. The broad registry wording cannot support a route-wide
  no-serious-harm claim.
- **Conflict of interest:** Three authors disclose a patent related to the study,
  and one is Hemogen's founder.

## Mechanism, Safety, Affordability, And Access

- **Mechanism:** HGI-001 uses a lentiviral `HBBT87Q` transgene in autologous
  hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells to restore beta-like globin
  production without donor GVHD.
- **Safety:** Mobilization, apheresis, busulfan myeloablation, cytopenia,
  infection, organ toxicity, manufacturing failure, insertional risk, clonal
  dominance, and long-term surveillance remain material. Five participants at
  about three years cannot resolve rare or late harm.
- **Affordability:** Sponsor pages describe localized manufacturing, but no
  reviewed source itemizes equipment, technology transfer, product,
  conditioning, inpatient care, surveillance, or total delivered cost.
- **Access:** The Hong Kong phase 2 record remains not yet recruiting, has no
  listed study location, and has no posted results. Sponsor activity does not
  establish Indonesia authorization, payer coverage, access, or affordability.

## Hypothesis, Falsification, And Next Action

- **Hypothesis:** A prospectively defined, larger phase 2 cohort could strengthen
  this early clinical benchmark if it reproduces transfusion independence across
  genotypes with complete treated and evaluable denominators, conditioning
  toxicity, integration-site safety, manufacturing failures, and delivered cost.
- **Open question:** Will a larger cohort reproduce the five-person signal
  without unacceptable route-level toxicity, clonal risk, manufacturing
  failure, or cost?
- **Falsification criterion:** Deprioritize HGI-001 if a complete treated
  denominator shows non-durable transfusion independence, unacceptable
  conditioning or product toxicity, clonal dominance, replication-competent
  lentivirus, manufacturing failure, or delivered cost outside the program
  threshold.
- **Next decisive action:** Obtain the full paper and supplement through an
  approved route. Reconcile its treated and evaluable denominators with the
  aliased registry record, and extract serious events by conditioning versus
  product attribution. Then require a larger prospective cohort and itemized
  delivered-cost model before reconsidering the route.
