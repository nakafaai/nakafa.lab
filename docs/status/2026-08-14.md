# Research Status: 2026-08-14

Audience: general research planning
Status: regional long-horizon pediatric HSCT durability benchmark, not treatment advice

## Question

Does the full two-decade pediatric TDT HSCT report, PMID `42587379`, close the
durability and affordability gaps enough to move matched-sibling HSCT beyond
the leading affordable-curative route benchmark?

## Decision

`promote_hla_identical_related_hsct_to_long_horizon_regional_durability_benchmark_only`

The report materially strengthens the long-term regional comparator for
predominantly HLA-identical related pediatric HSCT. It does not change the
route ranking. Matched-sibling HSCT remains
`leading_affordable_curative_route_benchmark_only` because the study mixes
donor types and transplant eras, does not isolate matched-sibling outcomes,
contains unresolved outcome-label conflicts, and reports no delivered cost,
payer route, or Indonesia implementation result.

## What Changed

The August 13 source sweep could retrieve only title and citation metadata for
PMID `42587379`. The publisher now exposes the full free-access report. It
supplies a 205-child outcome denominator, donor composition, conditioning
groups, graft failure, mortality, transfusion-free status, long-horizon
survival estimates, and early toxicities from one Saudi Arabian center.

This closes the source-acquisition blocker and improves the evidence base from
an unresolved citation to a direct regional durability benchmark. It does not
close the contemporary matched-sibling, late-safety, or affordability gaps.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact - design and denominator:** The retrospective single-center study
  includes 205 children with TDT who received allogeneic HCT at King Faisal
  Specialist Hospital and Research Centre from January 1998 through September
  2024. Median age at transplant was 5.1 years, range 1.0 to 15.8 years.
- **Fact - donor scope:** Donors were 158 HLA-identical siblings, 42
  HLA-identical other relatives, four related one-antigen mismatch donors, and
  one HLA-identical unrelated donor. The whole-cohort outcome must not be
  relabeled as a pure matched-sibling result.
- **Fact - transplant route:** All participants received myeloablative
  conditioning. Bone marrow was used for 199 transplants and peripheral blood
  stem cells for six. The report groups 131 participants under
  busulfan-cyclophosphamide with or without ATG and 74 under
  busulfan-cyclophosphamide-fludarabine.
- **Fact - graft and durable outcome:** Twenty participants had graft failure
  after the first transplant. Five received salvage transplants, four of whom
  engrafted and remained alive. At last follow-up, 172 of 205 participants,
  83.9%, were alive and transfusion-free. Overall mortality was 19 of 205,
  9.3%, at a median follow-up of 100 months.
- **Fact - reported long-horizon estimates:** The article reports overall
  survival of 90.4% and thalassemia-free survival of 81.5% at a long horizon.
  The exact horizon is internally inconsistent and is preserved as unresolved
  below.
- **Fact - conditioning comparison:** Reported thalassemia-free survival was
  90.5% for the 74-person busulfan-cyclophosphamide-fludarabine group and 76.7%
  for the 131-person busulfan-cyclophosphamide with or without ATG group,
  `p=0.041`. Overall survival was 93.2% versus 89.1%, `p=0.451`.
- **Fact - observed burden:** Grade I or II acute graft-versus-host disease was
  reported in 37 participants, grades III or IV in 16, and chronic
  graft-versus-host disease in eight of 194 evaluable participants. Within 100
  days, 142 had hypertension, 53 severe mucositis, 29 CMV reactivation, 38
  bacterial infections, 37 viral infections, and eight fungal infections.

## Contradictions And Limits

- Table 1 labels the survival estimates as 24-year outcomes, while the article
  text calls the same 90.4% and 81.5% values 20-year outcomes. Use
  `long-horizon` until the authors or publisher correct the record.
- The table reports 10 graft failures at day 100, 20 after the first
  transplant, and 16 under the latest evaluation label `primary graft
  failure`. The prose reports 20 post-HCT graft failures and five salvage
  transplants. These time points and labels must not be merged into one rate.
- The article reports a median last-follow-up hemoglobin of 113 g/dL, range 91
  to 156. The source gives no clarification for that unit and no correction was
  found. The value is excluded rather than silently converted.
- Outcomes are not stratified for the 158 HLA-identical siblings versus the 47
  other donor types. The whole-cohort result cannot establish a matched-sibling
  rate.
- The conditioning comparison is retrospective across a 26-year enrollment
  window. Calendar era, donor selection, supportive care, pre-transplant
  status, and other confounding can explain part of the visible difference.
  The `p=0.041` association is not causal regimen superiority.
- Median follow-up of 100 months does not mean every participant contributed
  20 or 24 years. The main article does not expose a donor-stratified number at
  risk for the long-horizon estimates.

## Cure-Program Read

- **Hypothesis:** A contemporary, standardized HLA-identical sibling marrow
  program can preserve durable transfusion freedom beyond five years while
  reducing graft failure, transplant mortality, and late toxicity relative to
  this mixed-era regional benchmark at a lower delivered cost than
  individualized autologous gene-cell therapy.
- **Interpretation:** Promote the source to a long-horizon regional durability
  benchmark only. Do not promote the conditioning regimen, donor-general
  route, affordability, Indonesia access, or individual suitability.
- **Open question:** In a consecutive current-era matched-sibling cohort, what
  are donor-specific five- and ten-year overall and thalassemia-free survival,
  graft failure, mortality, graft-versus-host disease, infection, endocrine,
  fertility, secondary-cancer, and itemized lifetime-cost denominators?
- **Confidence:** High that the report adds a material 205-person regional
  durability signal. Moderate for transfer to contemporary matched-sibling
  practice. Low for causal conditioning comparisons, lifetime toxicity,
  affordability, payer coverage, and Indonesia delivery.
- **Affordability and access:** The route uses myeloablative multi-drug
  conditioning, a donor, a transplant center, admission, infection management,
  and long-term surveillance. The report provides no itemized cost, payer
  payment, Indonesia center denominator, procurement path, or capacity result.

## Falsification Criterion

Remove the new regional durability benchmark if an author correction materially
reduces the 205-person outcome denominator, changes the reported long-horizon
survival estimates, or shows that durable transfusion freedom is not preserved
in the HLA-identical related subgroup. Remove the leading affordable-route
label if an independent contemporary matched-sibling cohort shows material
loss of five-year thalassemia-free survival, unacceptable graft failure,
death, severe toxicity, or lifetime delivered cost that does not retain an
advantage over the closest curative alternatives.

## Next Decisive Action

Obtain author or publisher clarification for the 20-year versus 24-year
survival horizon, the day-100, first-transplant, and latest graft-failure
labels, and the hemoglobin unit. Then request donor-stratified and calendar-era
stratified survival, number-at-risk tables, complete late endocrine, fertility,
malignancy, and organ outcomes, plus an activity-based delivered-cost model.
Compare a current HLA-identical sibling subset with the 176-child LMIC cohort
and the narrow Indonesia execution benchmark. No patient action, referral, or
sample routing follows.
