# Finding: YOLT-204 Registry Disambiguation Gate

Date checked: 2026-08-20
Evidence label: clinical-registry benchmark, not treatment advice

## Direct Answer

YOLT-204 now has three traceable ClinicalTrials.gov records, but no official
source establishes that they are one combined study, three independent studies,
or a documented sequence of replacements and expansions.

| Record | Current role | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`NCT06678165`](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06678165) | TDT-specific adult pilot, protocol `YOLT-204-IIT`, planning 3 people aged 18 to 35. | Not yet recruiting; status last verified October 2024; estimated completion passed; excludes beta-zero/beta-zero; no posted results. |
| [`NCT07190001`](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07190001) | Pediatric mixed-hemoglobinopathy record, protocol `YOLT-204-IIT-002`, planning 18 people aged 3 to 17. | Not yet recruiting; status last verified September 2025; estimated start passed; eligibility is SCD-oriented; no posted results. |
| [`NCT07774351`](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07774351) | Adolescent and adult mixed-hemoglobinopathy record, protocol `YOLT-204-IIT-005`, planning 30 people aged 14 to 35. | Posted August 19, 2026; not yet recruiting; TDT definition is missing; no posted results. |

The decision is:

`hold_yolt_204_at_three_record_pre_results_systemic_genetic_intervention_benchmark_only_pending_protocol_identity_and_first_dosed_results`

Keep all three records separate and never sum their planned enrollment. CS-101
remains the stronger reported Asia editing benchmark. YOLT-204 remains a
registry watchlist signal, not clinical efficacy, cure, active execution,
safety, affordability, access, or individual-suitability evidence.

## What Changed

The August 19 posting of `NCT07774351` changes the earlier two-record map. It
adds:

- a third responsible institution and protocol ID;
- four planned intravenous dose groups at 0.6, 1.0, 1.25, and an unresolved
  mg/kg dose;
- an explicit ClinicalTrials.gov genetic-intervention classification;
- intended-modification outcomes in peripheral-blood leukocytes and bone-marrow
  cells; and
- primary outcomes for adverse events, sustained transfusion reduction, and
  sustained HbF of at least 20% through week 52.

The record strengthens the traceability of a planned systemic genetic
intervention. It does not show that anyone was screened, enrolled, dosed, or
followed.

## Three-Record Reconciliation

- **Fact - shared fields:** All records name YOLT-204, plan a single-dose or
  day-0 intervention, include 52-week safety follow-up, and measure transfusion,
  hemoglobin or HbF, and intentionally modified alleles.
- **Fact - nonshared fields:** Sponsors, protocol IDs, ages, phase labels,
  planned enrollment, dose detail, TDT definitions, genotype criteria, and
  estimated timelines differ.
- **Fact - stale execution fields:** `NCT06678165` and `NCT07190001` remain not
  yet recruiting after their estimated start dates. The first record also
  retains an estimated June 1, 2026 completion date. Stale dates do not establish
  dosing, failure, termination, or supersession.
- **Conflict - phenotype scope:** `NCT06678165` is TDT-specific and excludes
  beta-zero/beta-zero. `NCT07190001` names TDT and SCD but its positive genotype
  and severity criteria are SCD-oriented. `NCT07774351` names TDT and SCD, yet
  supplies no TDT diagnostic, transfusion-burden, or genotype definition.
- **Conflict - protocol relation:** ClinicalTrials.gov exposes no duplicate,
  replacement, extension, or successor relation. Similar wording and endpoints
  are insufficient to pool the records.

## Novelty Impact

This does not create a Nakafa cure claim. It raises the comparison bar. Future
candidates must show what they add beyond CS-101 and `NCT07489196` as the
reported and phase-2-escalating Asia editing benchmark, `VGB-Ex01` as the
integrated HbF, alpha-chain, hemolysis, and transfusion comparator, and YOLT-204
as the systemic genetic-intervention registry signal. HbF or transfusion-reduction
intent alone is not a defensible novelty claim.

## Operational Consequence

- Track all three YOLT-204 records separately.
- Do not collapse their planned enrollment or endpoint lists.
- Keep YOLT-204 in the editing and HbF registry-watchlist lane.
- Keep CS-101 and VGB-Ex01 as the stronger anchors until YOLT-204 has a verified
  first dose, posted results, a primary publication, or auditable protocol
  evidence.

## Mechanism And Safety Boundary

- **Fact:** `NCT07774351` describes intravenous administration, classifies the
  intervention as genetic, and measures intentionally modified alleles in blood
  and marrow.
- **Hypothesis:** Persistent intended modification in marrow and blood could
  increase HbF and reduce transfusion burden without autologous collection and
  individualized ex vivo manufacture.
- **Interpretation:** The official registry supports a planned systemic genetic
  intervention. It does not identify the editor, genomic target, complete
  carrier, marrow-targeting performance, preparative regimen, or mechanism of
  HbF change, so those details are not inferred.
- **Safety boundary:** Eligibility exclusions for oligonucleotide or lipid
  nanoparticle allergy, infection, bleeding, organ dysfunction, splenomegaly,
  iron overload, malignancy, and prior advanced therapy are risk controls, not
  observed adverse events. The new outcome list does not name off-target,
  clonal-expansion, malignancy, or long-term genomic-safety endpoints.

## Affordability And Access Boundary

The new record contains no product cost, manufacturing yield, monitoring cost,
hospital capacity, payer, family cost, Indonesia authorization, or access path.
Intravenous dosing alone does not establish a simpler or lower-cost route than
ex vivo gene-cell therapy or HSCT.

## Falsification Criterion

Revise the three-record label if official history or protocol documentation
identifies a duplicate, replacement, or extension relation. Do not promote the
lane beyond a registry benchmark until a TDT-specific prospective cohort reports
screened, enrolled, dosed, and evaluable denominators; protocol identity;
editor, target, carrier, and dose; intended and off-target editing; clonal and
malignancy surveillance; adverse-event attribution; transfusion and hemoglobin
outcomes; durability; and delivered cost.

## Next Decisive Action

Obtain public protocols or responsible-party clarification for
`YOLT-204-IIT`, `YOLT-204-IIT-002`, and `YOLT-204-IIT-005`. Compare sponsor,
site, age, TDT definition, genotype, editor, target, carrier, dose, preparative
regimen, first-dosed denominator, safety surveillance, transfusion endpoints,
long-term follow-up, and delivered cost. Do not merge planned enrollment while
those fields remain unresolved.

## Boundary

This finding does not recommend YOLT-204, gene editing, trial enrollment, or any
therapy for any patient. No patient action, sample routing, or treatment advice
follows.
