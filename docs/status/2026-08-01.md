# Daily Research Journal: 2026-08-01

Audience: founder / researcher
Status: GATAD2A-CHD4 interface promoted to a preclinical HbF probe benchmark;
resveratrol remains deprioritized from the first affordable-cure HbF quote

## Question And Decision

**Question:** Does the 2026 randomized Thailand ferritin result, read with the
earlier human resveratrol trial, justify keeping purified resveratrol in the
first affordable-cure HbF quote panel?

**Decision:**
`deprioritize_resveratrol_from_first_affordable_cure_hbf_panel_to_ntdt_iron_overload_comparator_only`.

The new result is material because it changes experimental spending: purified
resveratrol is removed from the active first HbF quote. It remains only an NTDT
iron-overload comparator pending complete randomized outcomes. The decision
does not change the curative-route ranking. Matched-sibling HSCT remains the
leading affordable-curative route benchmark only.

**Confidence:** High that current human evidence does not support an active
affordable-cure or HbF-panel priority. Low to moderate that the new ferritin
signal represents lower tissue iron because the result is a small conference
abstract and does not report organ iron or the registered labile-iron markers.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact:** EHA-3064 randomized 30 adults with NTDT on stable iron chelation to
  resveratrol (`n=10`), omeprazole (`n=11`), or placebo (`n=9`). Median
  six-month serum-ferritin change was -116, -8, and +8 ng/mL, respectively.
  Resveratrol differed from omeprazole (`P=0.016`) and placebo (`P=0.012`).
- **Fact:** The resveratrol group reported three adverse events. The abstract
  does not report HbF, hemoglobin, transfusion burden, liver iron
  concentration, labile plasma iron, non-transferrin-bound iron, chelation
  changes, durability after withdrawal, or delivered cost.
- **Fact:** ISRCTN `73258526` planned 60 participants and registered labile
  plasma iron, non-transferrin-bound iron, erythroferrone, hepcidin, and an HbF
  association as secondary measures. The record remained recruiting or ongoing
  when checked.
- **Fact:** PMID `29926158` randomized 54 adults with NTDT to hydroxyurea,
  resveratrol plus piperine, or their combination. The abstract reports no
  significant response-rate difference.
- **Fact:** The 2023 Cochrane synthesis reports a
  hydroxyurea-versus-resveratrol hemoglobin mean difference of -0.30 g/dL,
  95% CI -1.14 to 0.54, in 34 participants, with low-certainty evidence. Three
  participants required a transfusion, but frequency and interval were not
  reported. HbF was not reported for this comparison.
- **Fact:** The 2012 patient-cell study remains a direct preclinical HbF signal,
  but it describes conflicting 20, 40, and 100 micromolar exposures across its
  text, table, and figure caption. It is not controlled clinical evidence.

## Contradictions And Evidence Limits

- The 2026 registry target is 60, while the conference abstract reports 30
  enrolled participants. Neither source explains whether the abstract is
  interim, early-stopped, or the complete cohort.
- The 2026 abstract does not report the registered HbF association or other
  iron biomarkers. Serum ferritin alone does not establish lower organ iron.
- The 2018 human trial combined resveratrol with piperine. It cannot validate a
  purified-resveratrol quote item.
- The randomized human studies concern NTDT. Only half the 2026 cohort had
  beta-thalassemia. These results do not establish an effect in TDT.
- Ten resveratrol recipients followed for six months cannot establish uncommon
  toxicity or long-term safety.

## Cure-Program Implications

- **Hypothesis:** The ferritin signal reflects an iron-axis adjunct effect
  distinct from HbF induction. It becomes credible only if organ iron or the
  registered labile-iron biomarkers improve with stable chelation and no
  worsening of hemoglobin or transfusion burden.
- **Interpretation:** Do not spend first-quote capacity on resveratrol. Retain
  it only as an NTDT iron-overload comparator while waiting for complete
  randomized outcomes.
- **Open question:** Does the complete study reconcile enrollment and show
  organ-iron or labile-iron benefit without anemia, transfusion, or safety
  tradeoffs?
- **Affordability and access:** No source reports itemized product, chelation,
  monitoring, adverse-event, or total delivered cost. Oral administration does
  not establish affordability, availability, or an Indonesia access path.
- **Falsification criterion:** Reconsider HbF-panel priority only if a complete
  controlled report shows reproducible HbF protein or F-cell improvement plus
  hemoglobin or transfusion benefit, acceptable safety, durability, and
  delivered cost. If the complete iron study does not reproduce ferritin with
  organ-iron or labile-iron improvement, deprioritize the iron-comparator lane.
- **Next decisive action:** Monitor the EHA report and ISRCTN record for the
  full cohort, registered biomarkers, HbF, hemoglobin, transfusion outcomes,
  chelation stability, complete adverse events, follow-up, and itemized cost.
  Do not add a new resveratrol lab quote before that result.

## Late Evidence Integration

**Question:** Does the newly indexed GATAD2A-CHD4 work provide a specific,
testable MBD2-NuRD intervention path for the affordable HbF program?

**Decision:**
`promote_gatad2a_chd4_interface_to_preclinical_hbf_probe_benchmark_only`.

This promotes a structure-defined target and reproduction experiment, not a
molecule, treatment, or cure candidate. It does not change the curative-route
ranking. Matched-sibling HSCT remains the leading affordable-curative route
benchmark only.

- **Fact - mechanism:** PMID `42539289` reports that the GATAD2A CR2 helix
  binds the CHD4 C-terminal domain and recruits CHD4 into MBD2-NuRD. Prime
  editing of GATAD2A `L387P/L390P` or enforced intracellular expression of the
  CR2 helix disrupted that interaction and increased gamma-globin and HbF.
- **Fact - human-cell scope:** In HUDEP-2 cells, the edited condition reached
  39% HbF by HPLC versus 7% in the prime-editing control. In primary adult
  CD34-positive HSPC-derived erythroid cells, lentiviral CR2-helix expression
  reached 74% HbF versus 8.9% with the mutant peptide control. The paper does
  not state the primary-cell donor count and does not test beta-thalassemia
  donor cells, TDT, NTDT, transfusion burden, or a clinical endpoint.
- **Resolved contradiction:** The abstract describes about 40% HbF versus less
  than 1% in control HUDEP-2 cells, while Figure 3F reports 39% versus 7% by
  HPLC. The figure-bound HPLC values are retained; the abstract value is not
  used as the control result.
- **Fact - modality boundary:** The functional peptide experiment used
  lentiviral intracellular expression. Companion PMID `42282813` identifies
  low-nanomolar macrocyclic binders in purified biochemical assays, but reports
  no cell permeability, cellular HbF, erythroid safety, pharmacokinetics,
  formulation, manufacturing, or cost.
- **Fact - safety boundary:** Short erythroid differentiation markers were not
  materially changed, but edited HUDEP-2 cells had about 50% lower `HBB` RNA.
  RNA sequencing reported 89 upregulated and 35 downregulated genes, including
  embryonic globins `HBE1` and `HBZ`. The preprint is not peer reviewed and
  provides no long-term marrow, organ, genotoxicity, or in vivo safety result.
- **Hypothesis:** A selective, cell-permeant inhibitor of the GATAD2A-CHD4
  interface can reproduce HbF protein and F-cell induction in genetically
  distinct beta-thalassemia donor erythroid cultures while preserving globin
  balance, viability, maturation, and enucleation.
- **Interpretation:** The structure-defined interface resolves the prior
  target-level blocker enough to justify probe development. It does not resolve
  the therapeutic-modality, disease-cell, safety, durability, access, or cost
  blockers.
- **Open question:** Can a chemically defined inhibitor enter erythroid cells
  and reproduce the HbF effect without broad NuRD disruption or embryonic-gene
  derepression?
- **Evidence tier:** Unreviewed preclinical structure, biophysics, genetic
  perturbation, lentiviral peptide expression, and short human erythroid-cell
  evidence.
- **Affordability and access:** Avoiding ex vivo cell therapy is only a future
  hypothesis. No administered product, scalable synthesis, pharmacokinetic
  route, manufacturing package, delivered cost, or Indonesia access path
  exists in the cited evidence.
- **Falsification criterion:** Deprioritize the lane if a chemically verified,
  cell-permeant inhibitor cannot raise HbF protein and F-cell percentage across
  at least three genetically distinct beta-thalassemia donor cultures while
  preserving viability, maturation, enucleation, and alpha/non-alpha globin
  balance, or if transcriptome, safety, manufacturing, or cost boundaries fail.
- **Next decisive action:** A medicinal-chemistry owner must supply one
  identity-, purity-, permeability-, and cost-qualified cell-active probe. An
  erythroid-biology owner must then run a dose-response comparison against
  vehicle, mutant control, and hydroxyurea in at least three genetically
  distinct beta-thalassemia donor cultures, including HbF HPLC, F-cells,
  `HBG1/HBG2`, total hemoglobin, globin balance, viability, apoptosis,
  `CD71/CD235a`, enucleation, hemolysis, and off-target RNA sequencing.
