# Daily Research Journal: 2026-07-27

Audience: founder / researcher
Status: F-BMT haploidentical HSCT promoted to a conditioning benchmark only

## Question And Decision

**Question:** Does the new prospective 29-person F-BMT haploidentical-HSCT
result resolve the matched-sibling donor bottleneck enough to become an
affordable curative route?

**Decision:**
`promote_f_bmt_haplo_hsct_to_conditioning_benchmark_only_pending_independent_comparison_safety_and_cost`.

The regimen earns a prospective alternative-donor benchmark. Matched-sibling
HSCT remains the leading affordable-curative route benchmark only.

## Direct Evidence

- **Fact:** PMID `42489576` reports a prospective, single-center cohort of 29
  people aged at least seven with TDT who received haploidentical HSCT after
  fludarabine, busulfan, thiotepa, and melphalan conditioning.
- **Fact:** Two-year thalassemia-free and overall survival were both 93.1%.
  Neutrophil and platelet engraftment occurred in all participants, with no
  reported graft failure or mixed chimerism.
- **Fact:** Grade II-IV acute GVHD occurred in 17.2% and chronic GVHD in 6.9%.
  One sinusoidal obstruction syndrome event and three hemorrhagic-cystitis
  events resolved with supportive care.
- **Evidence tier:** Peer-reviewed prospective outcome evidence, but
  single-center, non-randomized, and reviewed at abstract level.

## Contradictions And Confidence

- **Registry conflict:** WHO ICTRP's `ChiCTR2300071890` mirror targets 30 people
  aged 3-25 and lists one- and two-year survival as primary outcomes. The paper
  reports 29 people aged at least seven and a two-year
  thalassemia-free-survival primary endpoint. The registry was last refreshed
  in June 2023 and has no posted results.
- **Independence gap:** The earlier 823-person multicenter trial included the
  same center and enrolled through 2023-07-31. The new cohort began on
  2023-06-01. The indexed abstract does not resolve participant overlap.
- **Safety gap:** The abstract does not report the number or causes of deaths,
  complete infection and severe-event denominators, ICU use, fertility or
  endocrine outcomes, or itemized delivered cost.
- **Confidence:** Moderate for a regimen-specific conditioning benchmark; low
  for comparative superiority, affordability, Indonesia delivery, or broad
  access.

## Interpretation, Affordability, And Access

- **Interpretation:** Haploidentical donation can reduce matched-sibling donor
  restriction. The result does not prove lower toxicity than the earlier
  protocol because conditioning, GVHD prophylaxis, endpoint definitions,
  centers, and populations differ.
- **Affordability:** The route retains HLA and donor workup, four-drug
  myeloablation, post-transplant cyclophosphamide, in vivo T-cell depletion,
  immunosuppression, infection support, and transplant-center care. None is
  itemized in the reviewed sources.
- **Access:** A China single-center result does not establish Indonesia
  transplant capacity, authorization, payer coverage, access, affordability,
  or patient suitability.

## Hypothesis, Falsification, And Next Action

- **Hypothesis:** In an independently enrolled multicenter cohort, F-BMT plus
  the reported GVHD prophylaxis will preserve two-year thalassemia-free
  survival while improving GVHD-free survival relative to the haploidentical
  protocol in PMID `41730859`, without increasing death, graft failure,
  infection, organ toxicity, or delivered cost.
- **Open question:** Are the 29 participants independent of the earlier cohort,
  and what complete safety, hospitalization, late-toxicity, and cost
  denominators accompany the 93.1% estimate?
- **Falsification criterion:** Deprioritize F-BMT as a conditioning benchmark if
  an independent comparison fails to preserve thalassemia-free survival, does
  not improve GVHD-free survival, increases mortality, graft failure, infection
  or organ toxicity, or exceeds the program's delivered-cost threshold.
- **Next decisive action:** Obtain the full paper and supplement through an
  approved route, reconcile the registry population and endpoints, and exclude
  overlap with PMID `41730859`. Then compare complete two-year survival,
  GVHD-free survival, graft failure, infection, organ toxicity, hospitalization,
  and itemized delivered cost across protocols.
